# Assessment of the model records: paper-fermenter-pressure-blind

Task `task-qtqm` (blind task of the headspace air pressure of an aerated fermentation tank). Marks proposed by an AI coding
assistant (Claude) from the run records; checked against the evidence and confirmed without change by one of the authors. The
items (a) to (e) are those of Section 5.6 of the manuscript. `L<n>` is line n of
`runs/<run>/transcript.jsonl`; a script is a file of `runs/<run>/workspace/`.

The agent saw the columns `y` (state and scored output), `x1`, `x2` (inputs) and `dy` (supplied derivative of `y`), the sampling period 5 s, and no unit or description. For the checker only: `y` is the headspace pressure in bar, `x1` the outlet valve position in % closed, `x2` the air flow rate in l/min. All errors below are in the unit of `y`.
