# Assessment of the model records: paper-turtlebot

Task `turtlebot` (described task of the TurtleBot 2 differential-drive mobile robot). Marks proposed by an AI coding
assistant (Claude) from the run records; checked against the evidence and confirmed without change by one of the authors. The
items (a) to (e) are those of Section 5.6 of the manuscript. `L<n>` is line n of
`runs/<run>/transcript.jsonl`; a script is a file of `runs/<run>/workspace/`.

## r01: recommended model `CalibratedMidpoint`

Expressions:
`x_next = x + Ts*(gf*v_f)*cos(phi + 0.5*Ts*(ga*v_a))`
`y_next = y + Ts*(gf*v_f)*sin(phi + 0.5*Ts*(ga*v_a))`
`phi_next = atan2(sin(phi + Ts*(ga*v_a)), cos(phi + Ts*(ga*v_a)))`

| Item | Mark | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| (a) interpretation | yes | Every term is interpreted in `discussion.interpretation`, and each interpretation is correct for a unicycle model: `gf*v_f` "is effective forward speed", `ga*v_a` "effective angular speed"; the translation term "adds a body-forward increment projected onto the world axes"; `phi + 0.5*Ts*ga*v_a` is "the midpoint direction"; `atan2(sin(.), cos(.))` is used "to wrap the angle"; `Ts` is the sampling period (parameter row). |
| (b) origin | yes | Claims checked: (1) projections and additive heading from differential-drive reasoning: plan before fitting at L15 ("Differential-drive kinematics with Euler and midpoint integration"). (2) "I tested Euler, midpoint and exact constant-command arc integration": `fit_models.py` written at L83, run at L87, output L88 with the leave-one-trajectory-out `x` RMSE 0.030063 m (`euler`), 0.029936 m (`midpoint`), 0.029943 m (`arc`). (3) Only midpoint kept because the errors were "almost identical": same three numbers of L88; the pool holds no Euler or arc model. (4) Gains "consistent with the observed reduced speeds": helper regressions at L47 (forward coefficient 0.80 on all transitions, 0.83 on settled block means) and L21 (heading-increment coefficient 0.58). (5) Half-step direction "follows numerical integration, not symbolic search": no search produced this term; the sparse regression of `residual_analysis.py` (L99 to L104) was run afterwards and concerns other candidates. No claim is contradicted. |
| (c) parameter types | yes | `Ts` (fixed): constant `Ts=.2`, fit_models.py:6, never passed to the optimizer. `gf`, `ga` (fitted): the two elements of `p` for `'midpoint'` (fit_models.py:11, velocities at :22-23), estimated by `scipy.optimize.least_squares` at fit_models.py:62. Values `0.8452284549388056`, `0.7107461893291924` equal `fit_results.json` (`midpoint`, `parameters`) and the printed line of L88 (`midpoint p [0.84522845 0.71074619]`). `make_submission.py` copies them from `fit_results.json`. |
| (d) reasons | yes | Criterion: joint residual in `x`, `y`, heading because it "constrains turning as well as translation, avoiding the weak angular identifiability of an `x`-only objective"; the weight 0.25 m/rad is declared "an analyst-chosen balance". Method: `least_squares` on rollouts because it "directly optimizes the required multi-step simulation rather than differentiating noisy logged poses or minimizing only one-step increments". Starting values `gf = 0.83`, `ga = 0.68`, "rounded from preliminary steady-command regressions on the training data"; these are the block-mean coefficients 0.8331 and 0.6817 of `helper_diagnostics.txt` lines 94-95 (L47, read by the main agent at L76). |
| (e) limitations | yes | Each limitation has a basis. Constant gains average trajectory-dependent changes: per-trajectory gains differ at L55 and L63. Midpoint omits the arc chord factor: structure; the largest turn per sample is about `0.71*0.95*0.2 = 0.135` rad. Only `0.000034 <= v_f <= 0.298156` m/s and `-0.948148 <= v_a <= 0.941161` rad/s observed, no reverse motion: data summary L35. Lag at command changes that a memoryless model cannot reproduce: first sample after a command change has a three times larger residual (L47, L104), and the task permits no delayed input. Odometry and actuator response not separable: only logged pose is available. Three trajectories and overlapping validation windows: data. The two RMSE values (0.030027 m, 0.029936 m) equal `fit_results.json`. |

The reason for the method in (d) justifies simulation-error fitting against one-step regression; it does not say why the trust-region reflective algorithm was chosen over another optimizer. The mark `yes` assumes that the first reason is sufficient.

## r02: recommended model `Euler`

Expressions:
`x_next = x + Ts*gv*v_f*cos(phi)`
`y_next = y + Ts*gv*v_f*sin(phi)`
`phi_next = phi + Ts*gw*v_a`

| Item | Mark | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| (a) interpretation | yes | Every term is interpreted in `discussion.interpretation`: `Ts*gv*v_f*cos(phi)` and `Ts*gv*v_f*sin(phi)` "add scaled commanded travel along the current heading"; `phi + Ts*gw*v_a` "accumulates scaled angular command"; `Ts` sampling period, `gv` forward gain, `gw` angular gain, which "absorb unresolved calibration and timing effects". The interpretations are correct for a forward-Euler unicycle model. |
| (b) origin | yes | Claims checked: (1) projections and heading accumulation from kinematics, no symbolic search: `plan.md` written before fitting (L16, family 1 "Forward-Euler unicycle"). (2) Initial diagnostics on pose differences and command-block phase: helper script and report, L40 to L57, read by the main agent at L69. (3) Comparison of "Euler, constant-twist arc, affine bias, free phase, odd cubic, smooth dead-zone, angular/forward coupling, heading harmonics and lateral-slip structures": the keys `euler`, `arc`, `bias`, `phase`, `cubic`, `smooth`, `coupled`, `harmonic`, `lateral` of `identify.py` (L73), all fitted and printed at L78. (4) Sparse angular regression as a diagnostic: `LassoCV` in `residual_analysis.py` (L89, output L94); no symbolic-regression package occurs in the transcript. (5) Residuals larger at command changes and correlated within blocks: one-step heading RMSE 0.087 rad in the first sample of a block against 0.018 to 0.021 rad later (L94); lag-1 residual correlation 0.92 and 0.96 in `helper_report.md` lines 70 and 147. (6) "held-out `x` error favor it despite inferior one-step heading residuals": pooled held-out `x` RMSE 0.029081 m, the lowest of the 15 family and criterion combinations of `investigate_results.json` (L86, L114); one-step heading RMSE 0.0430 rad against 0.0407 rad (`ExactArc`) and 0.0414 rad (`AngularDeadZone`) in `residual_results.json` (L94). |
| (c) parameter types | yes | `Ts` (fixed): constant `TS=.2`, identify.py:4; row written as fixed at deliver.py:16. `gv`, `gw` (fitted): deliver.py:15 calls `I.fit('euler',[0,1,2],weights=(1,0,0))`, which is `scipy.optimize.least_squares` at identify.py:55-57 with the specification of identify.py:10. Values `0.8322675435264847`, `0.7825774999174929` equal the output of `deliver.py` at L106 and L122 and `euler_xonly` in `investigate_results.json`. |
| (d) reasons | yes | Criterion (`x` error only over 25 transitions): "directly matches the scored output and intended 5 s horizon", and it "had the lowest pooled leave-one-trajectory-out `x` RMSE of the tested settings" (confirmed: 0.029081 m, L114). Method: nonlinear least squares because "ordinary linear regression cannot optimize this rollout criterion". Starting values `gv = 0.83`, `gw = 0.68`: "come from diagnostic settled-command gains"; these are 0.830 and 0.678 in `helper_report.md` lines 99 and 22. |
| (e) limitations | yes | Each limitation has a basis. Command ranges `0.000034312 <= v_f <= 0.298155603` m/s, `-0.948147536 <= v_a <= 0.941160627` rad/s, no reverse motion: data. Pose noise, calibration drift and slip not separable: only the logged pose is available. No previous command or actuator state, so command carryover is not reproduced: structure, with the observation of L94. Coefficients are effective predictors, not mechanical parameters: structure. Long-duration accuracy not established: whole-trajectory `x` RMSE up to 0.16 m at L114. `phi_next` is not wrapped although the logged `phi` is: structure. |

The text of `estimation.method` is shared by the four models of the pool (deliver.py:40): for `Euler` it mentions "small plausible offsets/thresholds", "dead-zone/trigonometric terms" and the units of `bw`, `dz`, `ell`, `q`, which this model does not contain. The items (c) and (d) are not affected; the checker may consider this for the request of `FORMAT.md` to write the record per candidate.

## r03: recommended model `CalibratedEuler`

Expressions:
`x_next = x+(Ts*gf*v_f)*cos(phi)`
`y_next = y+(Ts*gf*v_f)*sin(phi)`
`phi_next = phi+Ts*(ga*v_a)`

| Item | Mark | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| (a) interpretation | yes | `discussion.interpretation` covers the three expressions: they "retain the old pose and add a command-driven displacement projected along the indicated heading" (`(Ts*gf*v_f)*cos(phi)`, `(Ts*gf*v_f)*sin(phi)`), and "the angular expression adds an effective command-driven heading increment" (`Ts*(ga*v_a)`). The parameter rows interpret `gf` ("coefficient of `v_f` in effective forward velocity"), `ga` ("coefficient of `v_a` in effective angular velocity") and `Ts` (converts "velocity to a one-sample increment"). The interpretations are correct. The text is a template shared by the six models of the pool (build_submission.py:65), so that "the indicated heading" is not named as the current heading `phi`. |
| (b) origin | yes | Claims checked: (1) translation invariance, `cos(phi)`, `sin(phi)` and an angular increment proportional to `v_a` from physical reasoning: `plan.md` written before fitting (L16: "unicycle/differential-drive kinematics with Euler, midpoint and constant-command circular-arc integration"; "State translation invariance will be preserved"). (2) "This was the first compact fitted baseline": `euler` is the first family of `fit_models.py` (L81) and the first line of its output (L86); only data inspection (L28) and the helper's regressions on pose differences (L48 to L64) precede it. (3) "no structure search supplied its terms": no search tool occurs in the transcript before L81. The origin has no further claims. |
| (c) parameter types | yes | `Ts` (fixed): written as the constant `.2` at build_submission.py:48; `TS=.2` at fit_models.py:4. `gf`, `ga` (fitted): build_submission.py:47 calls `fm.fit('euler',[0,1,2])`, which is `scipy.optimize.least_squares` at fit_models.py:53-55 with the specification of fit_models.py:10; `fit_extensions.py` and `fit_rectified.py` replace `maps` only for other families. Values `0.850757732426143`, `0.7642459831649933` equal `fit_results.json` (`euler`) and the printed line of L86 (`euler p [0.85076 0.76425]`). |
| (d) reasons | yes | Criterion (squared errors of `x`, `y` and heading weighted by 0.2 m/rad over 25 transitions): "gives position priority while retaining heading supervision", and the output-error form "avoids treating noisy pose differences as exact velocities". Method: bounded nonlinear least squares "handles nonlinear rollout dependence directly, unlike ordinary least squares on noisy one-step differences". Starting values `gf = 0.83`, `ga = 0.7`: "Positive gains near the exploratory observed response were chosen"; the helper's block regressions give 0.835 and 0.680 (`helper_report.txt` lines 43 and 34, read at L77). The stated check of other heading weights was run (`diagnostics.py`, weights 0, 0.1, 0.4; L89 to L94). |
| (e) limitations | yes | Each limitation has a basis. Command ranges and no reverse motion: data (`helper_report.txt` line 3). Odometry is not independent ground truth: only the logged pose is available. No velocity or command-history state: structure. "Euler residuals at command changes strongly correlate with the missing previous angular command": correlation -0.977 between the heading residual and the command change in the first sample of a block, and heading RMSE 0.086 rad there against 0.017 to 0.020 rad later (`diagnostics.txt`, L94). Three trajectories, overlapping windows: data. Gains absorb timing effects: structure. The three RMSE values (0.02970447 m, 0.02968373 m, 0.13114437 m) equal `fit_results.json`. |

Two statements of `estimation.method` lie outside the five items and were checked: the SciPy documentation and the chapter of LaValle were fetched (L117, L119), after the text that says "consulted online" was written (L113) and before the files were produced (L121). ## r04: recommended model `RectifiedEuler`

Expressions:
`x_next = x+Ts*(gf*Max(v_f-df,0))*cos(phi)`
`y_next = y+Ts*(gf*Max(v_f-df,0))*sin(phi)`
`phi_next = phi+Ts*(ga*v_a+ba)`

| Item | Mark | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| (a) interpretation | yes | Every term is interpreted in `discussion.interpretation`: `gf*Max(v_f-df,0)` "is a nonnegative effective forward speed; `gf` scales the command and `df` suppresses small commands"; `ga*v_a+ba` "is effective angular rate"; `cos(phi)` and `sin(phi)` "project forward displacement onto the world axes"; "Euler integration uses the beginning-of-step heading"; `Ts` is the sample period. The parameter row of `ba` calls it "an empirical offset, not an independently established actuator bias". The interpretations are correct for the expressions. |
| (b) origin | yes | Claims checked: (1) "PLAN.md records the families before fitting": written at L16, first fit at L64. (2) Helper findings "little transferable forward nonlinearity, near-zero lateral slip and strong switching transients": helper reply L55 (cubic forward map held-out RMSE 0.01185 m/s against 0.01025 m/s affine; settled lateral velocity RMS 0.00049 m/s; first angular transition "dominated by the previous command response"). (3) Families compared: `linear_euler`, `linear_midpoint`, `cubic_turn`, `deadzone_turn`, `smooth_turn`, `wheel_deadzone`, `body_slip`, `coupled_turn` (fit_models.py, L60, output L65), `speed_saturation`, `speed_turn_loss` (refine_models.py, L68, output L73), `calibrated_theta` (theta_models.py, L76, output L81). (4) "The forward threshold was tried after affine fits produced a small negative low-command speed": the affine fits of L65 gave `bf = -0.0082` m/s; `speed_deadzone` first appears in the next script (L68). (5) "lowest pooled held-out `x` RMSE among the explored candidates": 0.02906545 m (L81); the next values are 0.02908078 m (`proportional_euler`, L81) and 0.029144 m (`linear_euler_x`, L73). (6) "Euler discretization outperformed anticipatory arc integration": with the `x`-only criterion, held-out 0.029144 m (Euler) against 0.029593 m (arc) for the affine map (L73), and 0.029065 m (`rectified_euler`, L81) against 0.029541 m (`speed_deadzone_x`, the same command map with arc integration, L73). (7) The two RMSE values of the text equal `theta_results.json`. |
| (c) parameter types | yes | `gf`, `ga`, `df`, `ba` (fitted): the four elements of `p` of the family `rectified_euler` (theta_models.py:5, :11), estimated by `rm.fit(fam,[0,1,2],(1,0,0))` at theta_models.py:25, which is `scipy.optimize.least_squares` at refine_models.py:22-26. `Ts` (fixed): the constant `.2` in the rollout of theta_models.py:18-19, not passed to the optimizer. Values equal `theta_results.json` and the printed line of L81 (`rectified_euler [0.87356029 0.78234471 0.006346 0.00419533]`). The starting values and bounds of the record equal theta_models.py:5. |
| (d) reasons | yes | Criterion: sum of squared `x` rollout errors "because `x` is the scored output". Method: nonlinear least squares because "ordinary least squares would not minimize the free-run criterion". Starting values `gf = 0.9`, `ga = 0.75`, `df = 0.008` m/s, `ba = 0.007` rad/s: "approximately unity speed gains with small offsets ..., based on exploratory block motion and physically plausible response". A reason is stated for each of the three. See the note below on the reason for the starting values. |
| (e) limitations | yes | Each limitation has a basis. Three trajectories, nonnegative `v_f`, stated command ranges: data (L55). No actuator velocity or previous input as state, so the switching lag is omitted: structure, with the observation of L55 and the one-step heading RMSE 0.087 rad in the first sample of a block against 0.018 to 0.021 rad later (`residual_report.json`, L89). Correlated residuals: lag-one correlation 0.18 to 0.51 per trajectory (L89). "The forward threshold is weakly identifiable near the small-command boundary": 15 of 450 samples (3 command blocks) have `v_f < df`, and the three leave-one-trajectory-out estimates of `df` are 0.0086, 0.0046 and 0.0076 m/s (`theta_results.json`). Euler integration neglects the rotation within a step: structure. `phi_next` is not wrapped: structure. Long-duration accuracy not established: whole-trajectory `x` RMSE 0.088 m (`theta_results.json`). |

Three points for the checker. (d): the stated basis of the starting values ("exploratory block motion") does not give these numbers; the helper's block gains are 0.835 and 0.69 (L55), while the starting values equal the rounded estimates of the earlier fit `speed_deadzone_weighted` (0.889, 0.743, 0.00797 m/s, 0.00655 rad/s; L73). The mark `yes` follows the rule that a reason is stated; `partly` applies if the reason must also agree with the record. (b): under the first criterion of the run (`x`, `y` and heading, L65) the affine arc model had the lower held-out `x` RMSE (0.030313 m against 0.030561 m), so the claim (6) holds for the `x`-weighted criteria only; the sentence "were not retained" is shared by all five models, although three of the named alternatives are in the pool. (e): the last limitation says that the angular gain "compromises between steady response and omitted transient response"; the fitted `ga = 0.782` is larger than the helper's settled gain 0.69 and than its first-transition coefficient 0.34 (L55), so the sentence is supported as a statement on the structure (one static gain for both regimes), not as a statement that the value lies between the two.

## r05: recommended model `calibrated_unicycle`

Expressions:
`x_next = x + ts*gv*v_f*cos(phi + ts*gw*v_a/2)`
`y_next = y + ts*gv*v_f*sin(phi + ts*gw*v_a/2)`
`phi_next = phi + ts*gw*v_a`

| Item | Mark | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| (a) interpretation | yes | Every term is interpreted in `discussion.interpretation`: "Forward body speed is `gv*v_f`; angular speed is `gw*v_a`"; `phi + ts*gw*v_a/2` "approximates the mean orientation during a sample"; the position increments are "its cosine and sine projections"; `phi + ts*gw*v_a` "integrates the angular speed"; `ts` is the sampling period. The interpretations are correct for a midpoint unicycle model. |
| (b) origin | yes | Claims checked: (1) trigonometric form and no lateral motion from differential-drive physics, no symbolic search: `PLAN.md` written before fitting (L18); no search tool in the transcript. (2) "Midpoint quadrature was selected after the measured transverse displacement RMS decreased from 0.000910 m with initial heading to 0.000176 m with measured midpoint heading over the 447 training transitions": helper output L41 (`audit_motion_results.txt` lines 51-52: 0.000910 m, 0.000176 m), reported to the main agent at L61; `fit_explore.py`, written afterwards (L68), uses midpoint integration for all families. (3) Families tried: `affine`, `power`, `poly` (turn-speed coupling), `asymmetric`, `deadzone`, `wheel` (fit_explore.py, output L73), `coupled` (refine_fit.py, output L85), `directional` (fit_models.py, output L101). (4) "small or unstable validation gains": held-out `x` RMSE 0.03185 m (`linear`, `x` criterion) against 0.03177 m for the best alternative (`wheel`, L73); fold estimates of the alternatives vary, for example the wheel mixing length 0.158, 0.079 and 0.130 m (L77). (5) Final windows start at every sample: `F.windows(ids,25,1)` at fit_models.py:20 against stride 5 in fit_explore.py:34. (6) The two RMSE values (0.029706 m, 0.029467 m) equal the printed line of L101 and `final_fit_results.json`. |
| (c) parameter types | yes | `ts` (fixed): constant `TS=.2`, fit_explore.py:7; written as fixed at make_submission.py:14. `gv`, `gw` (fitted): family `linear` with the criterion `x` (fit_models.py:16), estimated by `scipy.optimize.least_squares` at fit_models.py:24 from the specification of fit_explore.py:10 (imported at fit_models.py:7). Values `0.8324413022190719`, `0.7414717792795524` equal `final_fit_results.json` (`linear`) and the printed line of L101 (`linear x parameters [0.832441 0.741472]`). |

No claim outside the forward-only command regime or for long distances: data (`v_f` between 0.000034 and 0.298 m/s). The residual numbers (angular-speed residual RMS 0.420696 rad/s at command changes against 0.081139 to 0.094116 rad/s at the other phases) equal the output of `diagnose_final.py` (L119) and `final_residual_report.json`.
