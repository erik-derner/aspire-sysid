## r01: recommended model `FourierMap`

Expressions:

- `y_next = y + a0 + a1*(x3*cos(x2)) + a2*(x3*sin(x2)) + a3*(cos(x2)) + a4*(sin(x2)) + a5*(x3*x4*cos(x2)) + a6*(x3*x4*sin(x2))`
- `x1_next = x1 + b0 + b1*(x3*cos(x2)) + b2*(x3*sin(x2)) + b3*(cos(x2)) + b4*(sin(x2)) + b5*(x3*x4*cos(x2)) + b6*(x3*x4*sin(x2))`
- `x2_next = atan2(sin(x2 + (hb + hg*sign(x4)*Max(Abs(x4)-hd, 0))), cos(x2 + (hb + hg*sign(x4)*Max(Abs(x4)-hd, 0))))`

| Item | Mark | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| (a) interpretation | yes | Every term is interpreted in `discussion.interpretation` by its mathematical effect: `1` "supplies a constant increment"; `x3*cos(x2)`, `x3*sin(x2)` "first-harmonic input-direction coupling"; `cos(x2)`, `sin(x2)` "direction-dependent offsets"; `x3*x4*cos(x2)`, `x3*x4*sin(x2)` "first-order turning-input corrections"; `hb + hg*sign(x4)*Max(Abs(x4)-hd, 0)` "offset drift and an odd dead-zone response"; `atan2(sin(.), cos(.))` "wraps the next angle"; `y`, `x1` "enter with identity coefficients". The angle reading of `x2` is labeled as an inference: "treated as an angle in radians, inferred from its wrapping, not given by the task". The cross-axis coefficients are "permitted mathematically, without claiming a physical cause". No physical meaning is stated as fact in the interpretation. Outside it, `estimation.data` says "diagnostics show actuator-memory effects" (a physical word for the residuals after an input change, not for a term). |
| (b) origin | yes | Families listed before fitting (affine, polynomial, bounded or periodic): L15. Polynomial maps of degree 1 to 3 by ordinary least squares with held-out trajectories, `x2` errors 4.67, 1.33, 11.58 at degree 3: L16, L21. `x3*x4` angle response against an `x4` response ("angle u*linear" RMSE 0.0453 against "angle linear" 0.0369): L52, L57, `helper_nonlinearity_results.txt:12-14`. Midpoint projection reduces the lateral residual (RMSE 0.00018 against 0.00091): L65. Euler and midpoint linear, midpoint cubic, signed-power, dead-zone and cubic exact-arc maps fitted by free-running least squares: L80, L85. Coordinate and heading predictors do not improve held-out local errors (`cos(theta)`, `sin(theta)` and `y`, `x1` raise the held-out RMSE): L88, L93. Interior-increment regularizer: L96, L101. Fourier library written by hand, no search package: L104 (no call of a symbolic-regression package in the transcript). Dead-zone angle form from the scalar comparison of six forms (lowest fit RMSE, 0.01470, and lowest held-out RMSE on two of the three trajectories; the signed power is lower on the first): L60, L65, `helper_scalar_validation_results.txt:3-14`. Held-out `y` RMSE 0.0858 against 0.0897 of the rigid dead-zone map: L109, L121. No claim is contradicted. |
| (c) parameter types | yes | All 17 parameters are declared `fitted`. `reproduce_submission.py:13` calls `fit_fourier.fit(trajs,100)`; `fit_fourier.py:13` builds the start vector of 17 entries and `fit_fourier.py:21` estimates all 17 jointly with `scipy.optimize.least_squares`. Order `a0, b0, a1, b1, ..., a6, b6, hb, hg, hd` follows `p[:-3].reshape(7,2)` and `p[-3:]` (`fit_fourier.py:9-10`). The 17 values of the file equal those printed for the weight 100 at L109 and stored in `fourier_results.json` (key `100`). No fixed or tied parameter exists; the weights `0.25` and `100` are constants of the criterion, not of the expressions. |
| (d) reasons | yes | Criterion: "The coordinate criterion addresses accumulated trajectory errors; the local penalty discourages spurious nonlinear response curves"; the weight 100 was chosen by held-out error among 20, 100, 300 (L109). Method: trust-region reflective least squares, "appropriate for the nonlinear accumulated-angle residual and parameter bounds". Starting values: 14 coefficients from ordinary least squares on the measured transitions, `hb = 0`, `hg = 0.1667`, `hd = 0.09` "motivated by interior scalar regressions"; the full vector is given and equals `fourier_results.json` (`starting`). |
| (e) limitations | yes | No previous input in the expressions, so the transients after an input change are not represented (structure; one-step `y` RMSE 0.00512 on the 90 switch transitions against 0.00148 on the 267 interior ones, `final_diagnostics.json`, L121). Residual correlation at lag one 0.436 (L121). Heading error accumulates into the coordinates (structure of a free-running simulation). Three trajectories only (data). Prefix-to-tail validation worse than whole-trajectory holdout (0.198 against 0.086, L121). Units and inputs outside the observed range unknown (data). All numbers quoted in the text agree with L109 and `final_diagnostics.json`. |

A checker should look first at the terms `a3*cos(x2)`, `a4*sin(x2)` and their `b` counterparts: the diagnostic at L93 showed that `cos(theta)`, `sin(theta)` predictors raise the held-out error of the scalar regressions, and the origin states that this "motivated the interior-increment regularizer rather than extra state-dependent terms", yet the delivered library contains these state-dependent offsets. The origin reports both facts, so item (b) is not lowered.
