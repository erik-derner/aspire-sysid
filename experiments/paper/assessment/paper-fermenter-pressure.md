# Assessment of the model records: paper-fermenter-pressure

Task `fermenter-pressure` (described task of the headspace air pressure `p` of an aerated fermentation tank; inputs `valve` in % closed and `flow` in l/min; one delivered expression `dp = f(p, valve, flow)`). Marks proposed by an AI coding assistant (Claude) from the run records; checked against the evidence and confirmed without change by one of the authors. The items (a) to (e) are those of Section 5.6 of the manuscript, marked by the rule of `experiments/paper/README.md`, section "Marking rule", on the model record of the recommended candidate of every run. `L<n>` is line n of `runs/<run>/transcript.jsonl`; a script reference such as `fit_final.py:42` is a line of a file of `runs/<run>/workspace/`. No test result was read for this assessment.

The task states the physical requirements R1 to R5 of Robert Babuška in natural language (`TASK.md`, Section 5): R1, `dp` does not decrease when `flow` increases; R2, `dp` does not decrease when the valve closes further; R3, with zero flow and an open valve, atmospheric pressure (approximately 1 bar) is the equilibrium; R4, with zero flow and an open valve, the pressure tends toward atmospheric pressure; R5, with a fully closed valve the pressure cannot decrease when air is supplied. The harness (the software of ASPIRE) does not verify them. The second table of every run gives two marks per requirement. "Addressed in the record" (`yes` / `no`) is marked by the assessor: the discussion or a parameter description of the record says how the model relates to the requirement. "Satisfied by the delivered expression" is copied from `experiments/paper/assessment/checks/paper-fermenter-pressure-requirements.json`, written by `checks/fermenter_requirements.py`: the delivered expression with the delivered parameter values is evaluated on the grid `valve` 0 to 100 % in steps of 2.5, `flow` 0 to 31 l/min in steps of 1, `p` 0.9 to 2.3 bar in steps of 0.05; R1, R2 and R5 are checked for `p` from 1.0 to 2.3 bar, R3 and R4 on the whole pressure grid, with "open valve" taken as `valve <= 90` % (37 positions) and the tolerance 0.05 bar around 1 bar for R3. Zero flow and a fully closed valve lie outside the training data (`valve` 10.3 to 92.4 %, `flow` 0.10 to 30.8 l/min, `p` 0.99 to 2.19 bar; L25 of r01).

In all five runs the final parameter values were estimated on the training and the validation data together, and no run executed a symbolic-regression package.

## r02: recommended model `mixed`

Expressions:
`dp = ain*flow-(gopen*(weight*(1-(Min(Max(valve,0),vfull)/vfull)**3)+(1-weight)*(1-3*(Min(Max(valve,0),vfull)/vfull)**2+2*(Min(Max(valve,0),vfull)/vfull)**3)))*2*Max(p-patm,0)/(sqrt(4*Max(p-patm,0)+(rvisc*(gopen*(weight*(1-(Min(Max(valve,0),vfull)/vfull)**3)+(1-weight)*(1-3*(Min(Max(valve,0),vfull)/vfull)**2+2*(Min(Max(valve,0),vfull)/vfull)**3))))**2+tiny)+rvisc*(gopen*(weight*(1-(Min(Max(valve,0),vfull)/vfull)**3)+(1-weight)*(1-3*(Min(Max(valve,0),vfull)/vfull)**2+2*(Min(Max(valve,0),vfull)/vfull)**3))))-back*Min(p-patm,0)`

With `v = Min(Max(valve,0),vfull)/vfull`, `g = gopen*(weight*(1-v**3)+(1-weight)*(1-3*v**2+2*v**3))` and `z = Max(p-patm,0)`, this is `dp = ain*flow - g*2*z/(sqrt(4*z+(rvisc*g)**2+tiny)+rvisc*g) - back*Min(p-patm,0)`.
