Identification plan, written before fitting

1. Inspect both supplied trajectories, input coverage, pressure convention, derivative consistency and input-step timing. Never join the trajectories.
2. Try gas-storage balances `dp = a*flow - conductance(valve)*pressure_drive(p)`, with positive inlet gain and nonnegative conductance decreasing with closure. Pressure drives to try: signed linear excess pressure, signed square-root excess pressure, signed power law, and a compressible-gas pressure drive. Atmospheric pressure initially assumed 1 bar; inspect evidence for an offset.
3. Valve families to try: power of open fraction; exponential conductance with exact zero at full closure; sigmoid/smooth positive monotone curve anchored to zero at full closure; if residuals justify it, a monotone piecewise-linear conductance. These are competing interpretable constitutive laws, not arbitrary unconstrained polynomials.
4. Estimate starting values against the provided filtered derivative with scipy.optimize.least_squares, then estimate final coefficients by minimizing free-running pressure errors under the evaluator's 5 s zero-order-hold RK4 convention. Initially fit training only, use validation for selection and residual diagnosis; consider fitting both trajectories separately within one objective for final candidates if justified.
5. Compare trajectory RMSE, derivative residuals by pressure/input, relaxation stability and physical requirements R1-R5. Explore an additional physically defensible structure only if residuals suggest it. Avoid unnecessary complexity and enforce positivity/monotonicity where possible.
6. Deliver a small structurally diverse candidate pool with exact provenance, scripts, numerical settings, limitations and physical-requirement discussion. Run the submission checker.
