Identification plan (written before fitting)

Compare continuous-time gas inventory balances with positive inlet gain and nonnegative outlet conductance. Families: (1) linear pressure relaxation with exponential/equal-percentage or power valve conductance, (2) signed square-root pressure-drop/orifice discharge, (3) generalized signed pressure-power discharge, (4) compressible isothermal discharge, and, only if residuals justify it, a smooth monotone multi-regime valve conductance. Atmospheric pressure is fixed at 1 bar as stated in TASK.md. All sealed-valve models will have zero outlet at 100 percent closure. Fit derivatives for initialization and compare all-row free-running RK4 simulation error, using train for parameter estimation and val for structural selection. Inspect pressure-, valve-, flow- and time-dependent residuals before proposing extensions. No inaccessible test data will be used. Constrain inlet gains and valve slopes to physical signs. Prefer low-dimensional models over unrestricted symbolic search because the required monotonicities and atmospheric equilibrium can be encoded directly.
