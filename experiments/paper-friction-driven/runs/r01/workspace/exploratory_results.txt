INTERPRETATION AND DEFENSIBLE INITIALIZATIONS:
1. Basic moving balance `motor*u - gravity*sin(theta) - viscous*omega - coulomb*sign(omega)` gives physical starting coefficients above. Moving data do not identify static breakaway torque. Never equate a discontinuous sign friction law with a complete sticking model.
2. Positive angle-dependent damping above is structurally distinct and globally dissipative. exp multiplier is mathematical modulation, not an established mechanical explanation. The derivative evidence must be checked with open-loop trajectory simulation before accepting.
3. Angle-dependent Coulomb friction `-(coulomb + angle_coulomb*cos(theta))*sign(omega)` is defensible only if `coulomb >= Abs(angle_coulomb)`; unconstrained extension values above are screening estimates, not final coefficients. Fits that violate this must be projected or refitted. May represent changing friction load, but data alone do not establish that mechanism.
4. Non-sinusoidal restoring torque `-gravity*sin(theta) - gravity2*sin(2*theta)` is globally restoring on the specified interval when `gravity >= 2*Abs(gravity2)`; screening values above supply starts, not a proof of final validity.
5. Nonlinear motor `motor*u + motor3*u**3` must satisfy `motor + 3*motor3*u**2 >= 0` throughout required input domain [-10,10] V. Negative unconstrained cubic slopes cannot automatically be submitted; a monotone saturation `gain*voltage_scale*tanh(u/voltage_scale)` is safer, initialized with basic motor gain and voltage_scale=10 V (chosen domain scale, not estimated).
6. Quadratic dissipative drag `-quadratic*omega*Abs(omega)` has globally correct sign for nonnegative quadratic coefficient. A coefficient hitting zero means these train derivative residuals do not support that extension.
7. Near-rest sticking extension requires a deadband of net driving torque and a low-speed transition, fitted by simulation rather than extrapolated from selected moving data. Initialize dynamic friction magnitude from basic coulomb, static threshold at the same magnitude, and speed scale 0.2 rad/s (chosen close to encoder velocity grid). Validate carefully: a discontinuity or very steep regularization may be unstable with fixed-step integration.
Time alignment cannot be resolved solely from a small derivative RMSE advantage: centrally differentiated encoder targets are smoothed and highly quantized; input waveform is also smooth at the sample scale. A delayed or averaged regressor is a diagnostic, not an admissible additional state or a claim of a true physical delay.
