# Identification plan, recorded before fitting

Compare: (1) pendulum plus viscous damping and smooth Coulomb friction (`a*u-b*omega-c*sin(theta)-f*tanh(omega/v)`); (2) continuous projected static/Coulomb friction, using saturation of net force plus low-speed velocity (`F-Min(Max(F+k*omega,-f),f)` with `F=a*u-b*omega-c*sin(theta)`); (3) Karnopp-style velocity-band static friction with a dead zone in force; (4) extensions of the projected family with quadratic damping, a positive angle-dependent restoring coefficient, direction-dependent friction, or monotone nonlinear motor gain if residuals warrant. Linear and sine restoring laws will also be compared. These are physically constrained parametric identification rather than unrestricted genetic programming. Initial coefficients will come from linear derivative regression on moving training samples; final coefficients will be refined against angle trajectories using exactly the specified held-input RK4 integration. Validation will be used for structural selection, with its use documented. Residuals will be grouped by velocity, angle, voltage and direction to decide which extensions to retain. Numerical and physical constraints: exact kinematics, nonnegative restoring force coefficient over the specified angle domain, positive input gain, and stable low-speed damping under the specified timestep. No test data will be accessed.
